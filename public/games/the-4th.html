This module is being rolled out as a file you open on your computer. You do not need a Waterman College login.

On a computer

  1. Open the email or text from your manager.
  2. Download The-4th-Training.zip.
  3. Unzip the folder (on a PC, right-click → Extract All).
  4. Double-click Open-The-4th.html.

On a phone

  1. Download the zip.
  2. Open it so you can see the files.
  3. Tap Open-The-4th.html and choose Chrome or Safari.

Do the tiles in order: Learn, Practice, Check. Tile 4 is the chart for the floor. It is not a test.
